Help

  • Text size: is kinda self explanatory.

Display:

  • Show chords:is ticked by default, un-ticking hides the chords (may be useful for drummers)

  • Show line numbers: is off by default, turning them on you enter a sort of "edit mode".
    On the song page you will now see faint line numbers and an icon near "section headers" (verse, chorus, solo etc are each a section)
    • Clicking on the line number or section icon will open an editor where you can add your notes to that part in that song.

  • Show Notes: your own name is ticked by default so you can see your notes (no need to show line numbers if not "editing") you can also view other peoples notes by ticking them on.

  • Big Tempo: this is again mainly for drummers, instead of just the tempo "LEDs" on the page, the page border also flashes.
    FYI tapping on the "LEDs" starts the tempo flashes.

  • Tempo Sound: adds a click to the "LED" flashes

Scroll behaviour:

  • Auto this will display or not the autoscroll overlay, if displayed the controls should be self explanatory, the default scroll speed can be updated and saved per song.

  • Snapping this will set how far from the top of the screen a section (verse/chorus/solo etc) needs to be in order to snap to the top of screen after swiping.
